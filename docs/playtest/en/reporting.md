# Filing a playtest report

1. **Remove names.** Replace every name with a letter (A, B, …). Leave out anything that identifies a person, a class or a family, and any photo with faces.
2. **Open a report.** On GitHub, open a new issue from the *Playtest report* template: <https://github.com/usnea-communitas/le-jardin/issues/new?template=playtest.md>. Its sections are:
   - **Group** — roughly how many people, age range, setting (class, workshop, family…), devices and language;
   - **What worked**;
   - **What confused people** — the places where players hesitated, with the act from the observation sheets;
   - **Which lesson landed, and which did not**;
   - **Suggestions**.
   Add the questionnaire totals (how many answered 1–5 for each line) and the answers to the open questions, word for word but without names.
3. **Link it.** Comment on [issue #8](https://github.com/usnea-communitas/le-jardin/issues/8) with the number of your report.
4. **Bugs separately.** Each bug gets its own issue from the *Bug report* template, with the device, browser, language and what happened.

Issues are public. If you are unsure whether something identifies a person, leave it out — the project needs the pattern, not the person.
