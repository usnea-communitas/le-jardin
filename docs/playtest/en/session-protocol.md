# Playtest session plan

For a facilitator running Le Jardin with a group, about 60–90 minutes. One person facilitates; if you can, a second person observes and fills in the [observation sheet](observation-sheet.md). We are testing the game, not the players.

## Before the session (about 20 minutes)

- **Devices.** One device per player or per pair. A laptop or a tablet gives the reference experience; phones work in portrait and landscape. Charge everything.
- **Connection.** Open the workshop link <https://permaculture-garden.vercel.app/?workshop=1> on every device and let the opening card appear once. Where the signal is weak, choose *Play without a connection* on each device first (the arrow in the header; on a phone, the ••• menu).
- **Language.** Pick the language in the game's language menu before the players start. Czech has full voice narration; the other languages show their text with English voice.
- **Sound.** Headphones, or sound off in a shared room (the *Sound on / Sound off* button in the header).
- **A clean garden.** The workshop link keeps its garden apart from anyone's own game on the device, and *Workshop* → *Prepare for the next group* clears it between groups. The [facilitator guide](../../facilitator/README.md) explains workshop mode.
- **The Living Lesson.** With the workshop link it always shows a reviewed lesson labelled *Garden wisdom offline* and calls no AI. To playtest the AI reflection itself, use the ordinary link instead and reset the garden by hand afterwards (menu → *Reset garden*); note which kind of lesson appeared.
- **Print** one observation sheet per observed player or pair, one [questionnaire](player-questionnaire.md) per player and the [consent text](consent.md).

## Opening (5 minutes)

- Say what this is: "We are testing the game. Nothing you do can be wrong; if something is confusing, that is exactly what we need to know."
- Invite players to think aloud if they are comfortable with it.
- Collect consent (for players under 18 also from a parent or guardian, following the rules of your organisation and country).
- Mention that the game has no timers and nothing to lose: a wrong answer gives a clue and another try.

## Play (30–45 minutes)

- Let everyone start with the opening story; they may skip it (*Skip story*). Note who skips.
- **Help only when asked, or after about two minutes of being stuck.** When you help, write down what you said: that is a finding.
- The guided journey has six acts. Note when each player reaches each one and where they hesitate:
  1. *Living soil* — compost or mulch on bare ground;
  2. *Every drop* — rain harvesting, then a growing area nearby;
  3. *Useful abundance* — something edible growing;
  4. *Room for others* — flowers, a bug hotel or a bird house;
  5. *Fair share* — sharing a real surplus from the harvest basket at the community table;
  6. *Learn through change* — turning the season and solving one garden challenge.
- Worth watching: do players understand that the garden scores **relationships** between elements, not single objects? That gifts build a shared fund and goodwill and **never turn into personal money**? Do they find *Next season*? Do they read the clue after a wrong answer and try again?

## Discussion (15–20 minutes)

Ask open questions and let the group answer before you add anything:

1. What did your garden need first, and how did you know?
2. Which connection between two elements surprised you?
3. What happened when you shared from your harvest? Why do you think gifts cannot become money in this game?
4. What changed when the season turned?
5. Where did you get stuck, and what helped you move on?
6. If you could change one thing in the game, what would it be?

## Questionnaire (5–10 minutes)

Hand out the [player questionnaire](player-questionnaire.md). It is anonymous; collect it face down.

## After the session (about 30 minutes)

- On shared devices: *Workshop* → *Save as a file* if you want the session log, then *Prepare for the next group*.
- Remove names from all notes, then file the report as described in [reporting.md](reporting.md).
- Report bugs separately, each with the device, browser and language.

|---|---|---|
| Opening and consent | 5 | 10 |
| Play | 30 | 45 |
| Discussion | 15 | 20 |
