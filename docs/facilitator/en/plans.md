# Session plans and the closing conversation

## Questions for the closing conversation

Ask, wait, and let the group answer before you add anything. Choose three or four.

**Design connections**

- Which two elements in your garden help each other? What does each one give, and what does it need?
- What is the difference between a useful pair and a full plant guild?
- Which connection surprised you, and how did you find it?

**Fair share and the community table**

- What are shares in this game, and what are they not?
- Why does a gift never buy anything back here? Was that fair?
- What did the community table do for your garden, and was it ever promised?
- Where around you do things circulate as gifts rather than purchases?

**Seasons and challenges**

- What changed when the season turned?
- Which challenge made you change your mind, and what was the clue?

**Back to a real place**

- Which relationship from your garden would you try first in a real one, and why?
- What could you do this week: cover one patch of bare soil, watch where one rainfall goes, leave one quiet corner for wild neighbours?

## 45 minutes

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–5 | Welcome. Ask: *What does a garden need besides plants?* Collect a few answers. |
| 5–8 | Open the workshop link on the devices. Choose **the third act** as the start point. |
| 8–30 | Groups of two or three play the third, fourth and fifth acts. Walk round with *Workshop*: ask each group its act's question. |
| 30–37 | Everyone turns to the next season and meets a challenge (the sixth act). |
| 37–45 | Closing conversation: one question on connections, one on fair share, one on a real place. Save or print the logs if you want them. |

Starting at the third act leaves soil and water already in place, so a short session reaches wild neighbours and the community table. Name the first two acts in the welcome so the group knows where the garden began.

## 90 minutes

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Welcome and the opening story, shown on one screen for everyone. |
| 10–15 | Open the workshop link. Start point: **the first act** (the story has been seen). |
| 15–40 | The first four acts. After the second act, stop for two minutes: *Where does the rain go now?* |
| 40–55 | The fifth act: the first gift. Talk about shares, the shared fund and why a gift buys nothing back. |
| 55–75 | The sixth act and further seasons. Groups solve challenges and tell each other the clues they found. |
| 75–90 | Closing conversation (four questions). Groups that reached the flourishing ending save or print their certificate. Save or print the logs. |

## 120 minutes

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0–10 | Welcome. Each group starts with **the opening story** on its own device. |
| 10–40 | The first three acts, at each group's own pace. |
| 40–55 | Break, or a walk outside: find bare soil, a gutter, a place where rain soaks in or runs away. |
| 55–75 | The fourth and fifth acts: wild neighbours, the first gift, the community table. |
| 75–100 | Seasons and challenges. The flourishing ending needs a full year, every living layer at 45% or more, two active connections, two gifts, a harvest and three different challenges. |
| 100–120 | Closing conversation, certificates for the groups that reached the flourishing ending, saved or printed logs. |

## If something goes wrong

- **A group does not know what to do.** The card at the top left (on a tablet or phone, under *Build*) shows the current act and what it asks for. The question for that act in *Workshop* often helps more than an instruction.
- **A challenge feels stuck.** A wrong choice gives a clue; read it together. A challenge can also be left for later and comes back.
- **The Living Lesson does not show an AI answer.** In workshop mode it always answers from the game's reviewed offline lessons. That is intended.
- **The sound is too much.** The speaker button in the header switches it off for that device.
- **The page will not load.** Without a connection the game opens only on a device where it was downloaded beforehand (*Play without a connection*); otherwise reopen the link once the device is online. The workshop garden stays where it was.
- **The next group arrives.** *Workshop* → *Prepare for the next group*. Anyone's own game on the device stays as it is.
