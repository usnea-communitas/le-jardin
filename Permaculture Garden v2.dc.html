1
Build the garden. Drag compost, beds, water and habitats onto the plot. Every element teaches you why it matters.
2
Share, don't just spend. Build through community barter (♻), then give surplus until the sharing goal reaches 6/6. If you give away a garden layer, grow it back.
3
Take what you need — not more. Neighbours leave surplus on the sharing table. Taking only what your garden needs keeps abundance flowing for everyone.
4
Pair up elements — discover guilds. ✦ In permaculture, good neighbours help each other. Place related elements side by side and see what they create together. There are 7 to find!
